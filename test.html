Bec me Agent Steve

The nebulite heist is shattering the world, the gravity is not functioning properly anymore and every step becomes a maze, borders disappear. With the use of the sun fragments, they plan to rewrite the fabric of the universe according to their own desires. The fate of the solar system and the entire galaxy hangs in the balance.

As you step into the shoes of Agent Steve, you find yourself in a dire situation with the solar system teetering on the edge of collapse. Humanity's survival hangs by a thread. But Steve isn't your average agent; he's a master of the paranormal and a specialist in unraveling spacetime mysteries. Get ready to channel your inner Agent Steve, brace yourself for an epic adventure, and dive headfirst into a cosmic journey to save humanity from impending doom.
